About Smart Transport Demand Prediction

A student project for exploring inter-city passenger demand across selected Chhattisgarh routes.

Project objective

Estimate passenger demand, classify expected crowd as Low, Medium, High, or Very High, and help users compare journey timing before booking.

Routes

Durg, Raipur, Bilaspur, and Rajnandgaon.

Data

Designed for travel date/time, route, weather, vehicle type, fare, and passenger booking information.

Technology

Python, Flask, SQLite, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and scikit-learn for planned ML integration.

Prediction method

A scikit-learn Random Forest predicts seats booked; the app converts it to a demand percentage and a Low / Medium / High / Very High crowd level. See the Model Status page for measured metrics.
